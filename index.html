The Process

How We Verify Every Signature

Collecting only works if you can trust what you're holding. Here's exactly how a signed piece gets to your door.

  1. Sourced directly

    Every item is sourced from licensed signing events or verified in-person appearances — never resold from unknown chains of custody.

  2. Witnessed and logged

    Each autograph session is logged with photo/video proof at the time of signing.

  3. Certificate issued

    A numbered certificate of authenticity is generated and permanently linked to that specific item.

  4. Sealed for shipping

    Signed pieces are bagged, boarded, or framed-ready before they ever leave our hands.
